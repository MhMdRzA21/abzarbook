Compress PDF to 100 KB

Many online forms cap uploads at 100 KB, usually for a single ID page, signature or certificate scan.

Drop your PDF here, or click to browse

.pdf

    What happens to your PDF

    Text stays sharp text. Only the images inside the PDF are scaled down, in steps, until the file fits under 100 KB.

    Tip: Scan in grayscale at around 150 dpi before uploading. A colour 300 dpi page can be over 2 MB and has to be downsampled hard to fit.

    Questions

    Will my PDF still look good at 100 KB?

    One or two scanned pages usually fit. A 30-page document in 100 KB leaves about 3 KB per page, which blurs images, so split it first.

    What if it can't get under 100 KB?

    You get the smallest version we could make, and the page tells you it didn't reach 100 KB. Splitting the PDF into parts usually solves it.

    Is my PDF stored anywhere?

    No. The upload is processed, handed back to you, and deleted from the server right after. See the privacy policy for details.
