Compress image to 100 KB

Profile pictures and ID photos commonly have a 100 KB limit.

Drop your image here, or click to browse

.jpg .png .webp

    What happens to your image

    The image is saved as a JPG. Quality is lowered only as far as needed; if that is not enough, the dimensions are reduced a little at a time.

    Tip: Square-crop first if the site asks for a square image; the tool never crops for you.

    Questions

    Will my image still look good at 100 KB?

    Photos fit with little visible change at typical screen sizes.

    What if it can't get under 100 KB?

    You get the smallest version we could make, and the page tells you it didn't reach 100 KB. Cropping first, then compressing again, usually solves it.

    Is my image stored anywhere?

    No. The upload is processed, handed back to you, and deleted from the server right after. See the privacy policy for details.
