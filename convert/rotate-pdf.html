    What happens to your file

    The page rotation setting is changed inside the PDF. Text and images are not re-rendered, so quality stays exactly the same. The upload is deleted from the server right after you download the result.

    Questions

    Does rotating reduce quality?

    No. Only the page's orientation flag changes. Nothing is re-encoded.

    Can I rotate just one page?

    Yes. Type its number in the Pages box, for example 4, or a range like 2-6.

    Why does it say my PDF is password-protected?

    Protected files can't be edited. Remove the password with the Unlock PDF tool first.
