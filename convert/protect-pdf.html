Protect a PDF with a password

Add a password so only people who know it can open the file.

There is no way to recover a forgotten password. Keep it somewhere safe.

Drop a PDF here, or click to browse

One file, up to 25MB

.pdf

    What happens to your file

    The PDF is encrypted with AES-256. The password is used once to encrypt the file and is not stored or logged. The upload is deleted from the server right after you download the result.

    Questions

    Is the password sent over the internet?

    Yes, to our server, so use this tool only on a site served over HTTPS. For highly sensitive files, protect them in software on your own computer.

    Can I recover a lost password?

    No. Without the password the file can't be opened, by us or anyone else.

    Will it work in every PDF viewer?

    AES-256 is supported by current versions of Adobe Reader, Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Preview. Very old viewers may not open it.
