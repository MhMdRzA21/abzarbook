    How it works

    1. Add your PDFs

    Drag and drop two or more PDF files, or click to browse and select them.

    2. Merge

    Every page from every file is combined into one PDF, in the order the files were added.

    3. Download

    The merged PDF downloads straight to your device, ready to share or print.

    Frequently asked questions

    In what order are the PDFs combined?

    In the order you add or drop them — reorder your selection before converting if you need the pages in a different sequence.

    How many PDFs can I merge at once?

    Up to 20 files per merge, each up to 25MB.

    Does merging affect PDF quality?

    No — pages are copied into the combined file as they are, with no re-rendering or compression.

    When merging is the right job

    Use merge when you already have finished PDFs and only need them in one file: a cover letter plus a resume, signed pages from different people, or a scan of receipts for expenses. Pages are copied as they are — no recompression, no rotation, no overlay.

    Merging does not make a file smaller. If Gmail or Outlook will reject the result, compress the merged file afterward with Compress files. Password-protected PDFs have to be unlocked in the program that created them before they can be merged.
