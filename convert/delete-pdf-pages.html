Delete pages from a PDF

Remove blank scans, cover sheets or anything you don't want to send. Type the page numbers and download the rest.

Use commas for single pages and a dash for ranges. At least one page must remain.

Drop a PDF here, or click to browse

One file, up to 25MB

.pdf

    What happens to your file

    The chosen pages are dropped and the remaining pages are copied unchanged into a new PDF. The upload is deleted from the server right after you download the result.

    Questions

    How do I know which page numbers to enter?

    Open the PDF in your viewer and use the page number shown there. Page 1 is the first page of the file, even if the printed number differs.

    Can I undo it?

    Your original is never changed. Download gives you a new file, so keep the original if you may need those pages back.

    Can I delete every page?

    No. At least one page has to stay.
